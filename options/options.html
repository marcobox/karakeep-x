Post Keeper for Karakeep

Unofficial. Saves a post from your logged-in X tab to your Karakeep. It talks to one Karakeep address only, and asks for permission to reach that address when you save it here.

Create a key just for this extension. Scoped keys need bookmarks: read & write and assets: read & write. The key stays in this browser profile's extension storage; it is sent only to the address above.
